How Hybrid Score works

01
Enter any discipline

Submit push-ups, pull-ups, or 5K. You can do one or all three — more disciplines means a stronger profile.

02
Earn 0–1000 points

Each discipline converts your performance to a point score on a curve. A stronger result = more points.

03
Score is averaged

Your Hybrid Score is the average across all disciplines you entered. This rewards breadth, not just one strong event.

Proof makes it official. This calculator shows an estimate. Only submissions with verified proof (video or race result) earn official leaderboard status and count toward your real Hybrid Score.
