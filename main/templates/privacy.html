Privacy Policy

Your data should be as clear as your rank.

EarnedClub is a hybrid fitness leaderboard for athletes who submit performances, build a Hybrid Score, join challenges, and prove real progress.

Contact: earnedclub1@gmail.com

1. What EarnedClub Is

EarnedClub helps athletes submit push-up, pull-up, and 5K results, appear on open leaderboards, claim athlete profiles, join challenge rooms, and earn official platform status through verification.

2. Data We Collect

  • Username, display name, email, age, and country.
  • Profile images and public profile details you choose to add.
  • Submitted performances, Hybrid Score data, ranks, badges, leaderboard position, and verification status.
  • Uploaded proof such as videos, race screenshots, proof links, or profile-related media.
  • Cookies, session data, login state, and basic technical/server data such as request logs and device/browser information where applicable.

3. Why We Use Data

We use data to create accounts, calculate leaderboard rankings and Hybrid Score, review proof, connect challenge room entries, keep profiles claimable, protect the platform, and improve the service.

4. Public Data

EarnedClub is built around public athletic status. Your display name, profile image, country, submitted performances, Hybrid Score, ranks, badges, leaderboard position, verification status, comparisons, and challenge participation may be visible publicly.

5. Verification And Proof

Proof uploads may be reviewed by EarnedClub staff/admins. Proof is used to decide whether a performance becomes official on the platform. Do not upload private content you do not want reviewed for verification.

6. Cookies

EarnedClub may use cookies or similar storage for login, session handling, analytics, platform security, and improving the experience. The cookie banner lets you accept this basic use without a large preference manager.

7. Deletion Requests

You may request account or data deletion by contacting earnedclub1@gmail.com. Some records may be kept where needed for security, moderation, dispute handling, or legal reasons.

8. Age

EarnedClub is not intended for children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account or submitted data, contact us so we can review and remove it where appropriate.
